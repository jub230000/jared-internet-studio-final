Why is audio production important?

I get how audio can be boring and not seeming as important as visuals. However, sound is essentially EVERYTHING! No movie can ever dream of expressing its intention without sound! Music would not exist. Comedies lose their punchline! Thrillers lose their suspense! Sound is everywhere and defines almost everything in media! It is up to producers like me to ensure that comes across strongly!
